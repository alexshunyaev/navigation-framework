\section{Optional Extensions}

I implemented two of the optional extensions, both of which feed directly into the MPPI cost
function.

\paragraph{Probabilistic Mapping.} The Gaussian risk field replaces
the binary obstacle test with a continuous one. In the planner the discrete ``infinite cost
inside the safety radius'' rule becomes a smooth penalty on the risk along a trajectory, with
a hard cutoff only at $\rho\ge\rho_\text{thresh}=0.5$. This gives the optimiser a gradient
that steers it away from obstacles \emph{before} a collision is imminent. Concretely, the
obstacle term averages a soft risk penalty over the horizon and adds a large finite penalty
for any sample that breaches the threshold, so guidance never freezes the robot yet true
collisions are ruled out.

\paragraph{Multi-Objective Optimization.} I add two objectives to the cost: a
\emph{smoothness} term $c_\text{smooth}=\frac1T\sum_t|\omega_t|$ that penalises jerky turning,
and a \emph{path-deviation} term $c_\text{dev}=\min_i\|\tau_T-p_i\|$ that keeps the trajectory
endpoint near the global path $\mathcal{P}$. The planner therefore optimises several criteria
at once --- progress, safety, smoothness and path consistency --- which is what exposes the
trade-offs discussed in Section~5. Both terms are tunable independently through the planner
configuration, so their relative influence can be studied without changing the algorithm.
